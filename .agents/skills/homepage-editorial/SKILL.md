# Homepage Editorial

Use this skill for page copy, front matter, section organization, and content review in the homepage family. Read [the editorial reference](references/editorial-system.md) before writing.

Choose the site before editing. The personal site is a portfolio and essays; Geometry of Meaning is a cautious, run-backed research archive; Bivouac is concise open-source product documentation. Read that repository's `AGENTS.md` for its required front matter and source-of-truth rules.

## Preserve the voice

Write direct, plain English in short paragraphs. Lead with what a page, project, method, or result is; use specific nouns and verbs; let links carry supporting detail. Prefer a modest claim with its scope stated to promotional language, generic scene-setting, or empty headings.

Keep prose scannable: one compact introduction, clear second-level headings where the material needs them, and lists only for genuine sequences or inventories. Reuse the site’s existing content model and template before adding fields or a new content type.

## Accuracy and links

Do not infer a fact, version, release state, affiliation, measurement, or roadmap. Link to the primary source when a detail belongs elsewhere. Keep external links intentional; page-local links should use the site's established Zola form.

For research, distinguish recorded measurement, interpretation, and inference limit. For Bivouac, describe the current implementation and specification precisely, without promising a roadmap. For the personal site, use first person where it reflects Michelangelo's experience, not a generic corporate voice.

## Finish

Check title, description, date, filename/slug, template, and extra fields against the destination repository. Render through the normal Zola checks; review the prose in context rather than treating metadata validity as sufficient.
